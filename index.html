The Real Runner

Meet Squeektoshi

Squeektoshi is a male Syrian hamster who generates physical proof-of-work on Squeekchain's WHEELY9000 setup. Each pass of his wheel magnet records one unit of work, which SqueekCore anchors to Ethereum to seal new Squeek blocks.

Squeektoshi runs primarily at night (UTC), and his livestream usually broadcasts during UTC evening and night hours. During the day, the wheel usually rests — and SqueekRunner glides at a steady baseline speed.

Fair Mint · Manual Review

How to get on
the whitelist.

Full criteria in whitepaper ↗
  1. 01

    Qualifying run

    Play ranked mode on the living wheel. The server streams map chunks dynamically. Reach a verified score ≥ 100,000 and collect all 10 / 10 coins in the first 10,000 m.

  2. 02

    Submit application

    After your run, click Apply. On the form, paste your Sepolia address manually — no wallet connection or signatures needed. Submit within 24 hours.

  3. 03

    Post on X

    Post your unique application code on X and paste the post link. Our server verifies the post via public oEmbed to confirm your account.

  4. 04

    Manual review

    The team reviews applications against published criteria — this is an application with manual review, not a raffle. Approved addresses join the Merkle allowlist for a free mint.

Review criteria

Qualified run (100k+ points & 10/10 coins) · Active X account with application post · Sepolia address with on-chain history · Bonus: Squeekchain activity (Squeeks, $SQK) · Obvious bot farms rejected · Team decision is final.
