Four steps

How applications work.

Review criteria ↓
  1. 01

    Qualify in a ranked run

    Play on /play/. A quick Turnstile check starts a ranked run on the live wheel. Score 100,000+ points and catch all 10 coins. Coins hang at double-jump height, randomly placed in the first 10,000 m; miss one and that run does not qualify.

  2. 02

    The server checks the run

    The server streams the map in chunks during the run and recomputes the whole run from your inputs and its own wheel timeline. Only the server's score counts.

  3. 03

    Apply within 24 hours

    Paste your Sepolia address (no wallet connection), enter your X username, publish the ready-made post with your unique application code, paste its link and submit. The server checks the post through X's public embed service.

  4. 04

    Team review

    The team reviews every application by hand against the published criteria. Approved addresses go into the Merkle allowlist for the free Sepolia mint.

Published criteria

What the team looks at.

Bots and farms can create addresses and solve captchas cheaply. People reviewing applications cannot be fooled as cheaply. So we review by hand, against these rules:

  • QualifiedServer-verified run of 100,000+ points with all 10 coins.
  • A real X accountA real, active X account that published the application post.
  • A used addressThe Sepolia address has on-chain history (not a fresh empty wallet).
  • Plus: Squeekchain activitySqueeks, staking or $SQK on the address count in your favour.
  • No farmsObvious farms are rejected (clusters of addresses, accounts or networks; scripted input).
  • Final decisionsThe team's decision is final.

Clear terms

Rules.

  1. Free: no payment, purchase or staking is required or accepted to qualify, apply or mint.
  2. Sepolia testnet only: minting needs only free testnet gas from public faucets.
  3. Zero monetary value: SqueekRunner Hamsters are testnet collectibles for fun and experimentation. No cash value, no yield, no promise of return.
  4. One of each: one address, one X account, one post and one qualifying run make one application.
  5. Server verification: only runs the server recomputes and confirms count. Offline and demo runs never qualify.
  6. Manual review: the team reviews every application against the published criteria. Not a raffle; decisions are final.
  7. Schedule: application dates and the mint date will be announced on X.

Questions

FAQ.

Do I need a wallet to play or apply?
No wallet connection at all. You play in the browser, and to apply you just paste the Sepolia address that should receive the mint.
Is this a raffle?
No. There is no draw and no randomness. The team reviews every application by hand against the published criteria.
Why do I have to post on X?
A real, active X account is much harder to farm than an address. The post carries your unique application code, which proves the account is yours.
What if X does not confirm my post?
If X's embed service is temporarily unavailable, you can still submit; the team checks your post by hand. If X says the post does not exist, make sure it is public and the link is right.
Where are the coins?
Ten coins hang at double-jump height, one in each stretch of the first 10,000 m, at random spots. A single jump can never reach them. After 10,000 m there are no more coins.
I qualified but closed the page. What now?
Open this page in the same browser within 24 hours: the qualifying run is remembered. After 24 hours you need a new qualifying run.
What data do you keep?
Your address, X username, post link, a hash of your IP address (never the IP itself) and your run's input rhythm. Address signals come from public blockchain data.
What happens if the hamster wheel stops?
The game keeps running at its baseline speed. You can always play and qualify.
