Important Disclosures

Integrity & disclaimers.

Testnet Collectibles Only

SqueekRunner Hamsters is an experimental collection deployed exclusively on the Ethereum Sepolia testnet. The tokens carry zero monetary value, offer no financial returns, and represent no equity or passive income.

Independent Project

SqueekRunner and SqueekRunner Hamsters are independent community creations. We read public Squeekchain telemetry but are not officially endorsed by, affiliated with, or operated by the Squeekchain core team.

Open Source Art Pipeline

All layers, generative algorithms, PRNG seeds, and metadata generation scripts are open-source and reproducible in the project repository under art/collection/.
